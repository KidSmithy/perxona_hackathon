# 🏪 FamilyMart AI Virtual Clerk — Presentation Pitch Plan

> **Tagline:** Bringing the future of convenience stores to life with 3D AI avatars, voice interaction, and touchless left/right gesture control.

---

## 🎯 1. The Opportunity & Problem

- **Store Staffing Shortages**: Convenience stores face high turnover and late-night staffing challenges.
- **Tourist Language Barriers**: Foreign tourists in Tokyo struggle to ask about Japanese snacks, allergens, or hangover drinks.
- **Repetitive Inquiries**: Store clerks spend significant time answering common questions (*"What's in Famichiki?", "What helps after drinking?"*).

---

## 💡 2. Our Solution: FamilyMart AI Virtual Assistant

An interactive, 3D Virtual Store Clerk hosted directly on a kiosk screen or tablet:
- **Friendly Omotenashi Hospitality**: Greets customers warmly (*"Irasshaimase!"*).
- **Voice-to-Voice Conversation**: Customers simply speak their questions and hear instantaneous spoken responses from the 3D clerk.
- **Smart Product Recommendations**: Knows exact store prices, calories, allergens, and perfect food-drink pairings.

---

## ⭐️ 3. Core Features Showcase

### 🤖 Meet the 3 Specialist Clerks
Instead of a generic bot, customers can interact with 3 specialized store personas:
1. **M1 (Taro - Hot Snack Specialist)**: Recommends iconic hot foods like FamiChiki, Spicy Chicken, and Oden.
2. **M2 (Ken - Bento & Onigiri Specialist)**: Recommends fresh rice balls, SPAM Musubi, and Japanese curry.
3. **M3 (Ren - Beverage & Dessert Specialist)**: Recommends Ukon hangover drinks, FAMIMA CAFÉ lattes, and Soufflé Pudding.

### 🧠 Grounded Master Store Knowledge Base
- **Exact Prices & Calories**: Quotes exact JPY prices (¥) and calorie counts.
- **Smart Pairing Matrix**: Suggests complementary items (e.g. FamiChiki + Famichiki Bun or Green Tea).
- **Scenario Logic**:
  - *Late Night / Drunk* $\rightarrow$ Suggests Ukon no Chikara + Pocari Sweat + Hot Oden.
  - *Study Session* $\rightarrow$ Suggests FAMIMA CAFÉ Latte + Spicy Chicken.

### 🎙️ Full Voice-to-Voice Experience
- **Hands-Free**: Customers just click **`🎤 Speak`**, talk naturally, and the 3D avatar talks back immediately with realistic lip-syncing!

---

## 🖐️ 4. Highlight Feature: Touchless Left / Right Gesture Navigation

Customers switch between clerks using simple **Left** and **Right** hand gestures in front of the camera — smooth, hygienic, and intuitive!

| Simple Hand Gesture | Action | Avatar Navigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **👉 Gesture Right / Swipe Right** | Next Clerk | **M1 $\rightarrow$ M2 $\rightarrow$ M3** |
| **👈 Gesture Left / Swipe Left** | Previous Clerk | **M3 $\leftarrow$ M2 $\leftarrow$ M1** |

---

## 🚀 5. Business Impact & Value

1. **Boost Average Order Value**: Smart pairings (e.g. adding a ¥88 bun to a ¥230 chicken) increase cart size per customer.
2. **24/7 Multilingual Service**: Welcomes international tourists and night-shift workers effortlessly.
3. **Memorable Retail Experience**: Creates a fun, viral attraction inside FamilyMart stores.
