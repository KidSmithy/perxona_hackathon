Supported Hand Gestures:

  • Victory / Peace Sign (✌️): Swaps avatar to agent_vtuber_01 (VTuber Clerk)
  • Open Palm (🖐️): Swaps avatar to agent_classic_01 (Classic Clerk)
  • Thumbs Up (👍): Swaps avatar to agent_mecha_01 (Cyber Robot)
  • Swipe hand left / right (any pose): Cycles backward / forward through Classic Clerk → VTuber Clerk → Cyber Robot → (wraps around)

A held gesture must be classified above 65% confidence for several consecutive frames before the avatar swaps — this filters out one-frame misreads and flicker between two similar gestures. A swipe is motion-based instead: it looks at how far your palm moved sideways within a short rolling time window, regardless of hand shape, so it works at any camera frame rate and doesn't require a specific pose.
